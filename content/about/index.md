## Why we made this

Both of us have spent a long time doing the same job from different chairs - building
engineering organisations, hiring into them, restructuring them, and occasionally watching
them get stuck. And we kept noticing the same thing: the advice available in public is
either a conference keynote that ends before the hard part, or a slogan short enough to fit
on a slide.

The conversations that actually helped us were never like that. They were long, unhurried
arguments with someone who had hit the same wall from a different angle - where you get past
the principle and into *how much*, *when*, and *what did you do when it didn't work?*

So we started recording those. One topic per episode. We disagree on air fairly often -
about whether a team lead and a tech lead should be the same person, about whether bugs
belong in the engineering budget or the product budget - and those disagreements are usually
the most useful part, because they are where the real trade-off lives.

There is no sponsor, no affiliate link, and no product at the end of this. We recommend
books because we liked them.

## Season 1

Six episodes, released between February and July 2026: [leadership
levels]({{< relref "/episodes/01-leadership-levels" >}}), [tech debt]({{< relref "/episodes/02-tech-debt" >}}),
[organisational design]({{< relref "/episodes/03-organizational-design" >}}),
[security]({{< relref "/episodes/04-security" >}}), [hiring]({{< relref "/episodes/05-hiring" >}}) and
[AI]({{< relref "/episodes/06-ai" >}}).

They stand alone, but they were recorded in that order and do build on each other. Career
frameworks show up in three of them; the chapter-and-champions pattern appears first in
security and then again in AI; and by the last episode almost everything earlier is in play.

**Season 2 may come in 2027.** We are collecting topics and arguments in the meantime. The
best way to hear about it is to [subscribe on
YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@TechLeadershipDeepDives) or follow the podcast in your app
of choice.
