---
title: "Tech Leadership Deep Dives"
---

Most tech leadership advice arrives as a slogan. "Hire slow, fire fast." "Pay down your
tech debt." "Managers shouldn't code." The slogans are memorable and almost never enough
when you're sitting in the room and have to decide something.

We started this show because the conversations we found genuinely useful were never the
keynote ones - they were the long, unhurried arguments with someone who has run into the
same wall from a different angle. So we record those. One topic per episode, taken apart
until we hit the parts that actually generalise, with the books, frameworks and war
stories that shaped how we each think about it.

Every episode here has a written deep dive alongside the video: the concepts, the
frameworks, the references, and the full transcript - so you can read it, search it, or
send a colleague straight to the part that matters.
